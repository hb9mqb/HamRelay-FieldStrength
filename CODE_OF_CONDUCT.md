# Code of Conduct

We want a technically rigorous and welcoming project. Be respectful, assume
good intent, criticize ideas with evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, and make
space for newcomers. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate
misrepresentation of measurements, and publication of private information are
not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the
security/contact channel listed on the repository. Reports will be reviewed
promptly, confidentially and proportionately. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
